# Privacy: what information is needed
For most reports, only the problem and its location are needed. You do not need to give your name, phone number or email to this assistant.
Avoid putting personal details about other people (names, licence plates, neighbours' unit numbers) in a report unless a City staff member asks for them.
The City publishes anonymised 311 data as open data: the request type, date, ward and the first three characters of the postal code. Your name and full address are not published.
