# Tree trimming (pruning) for City trees
City-owned trees on the road allowance or boulevard can be pruned by the City's forestry team when branches block sidewalks, roads, signs or streetlights, or touch buildings.
Say where the tree is and what the branches are blocking. Trees on private property are the owner's responsibility.
If a branch is broken and about to fall, or a tree has already come down, report a tree emergency instead.
