# Green bin (organics) not picked up
If your green bin for food scraps and organics was not emptied on collection day, report a missed organics pickup. Solid Waste Management Services handles this.
Organics may be collected by a different truck than garbage or recycling, sometimes at a different time of day, so wait until the end of the collection day before reporting.
Keep the lid locked to stop raccoons, and put the bin out with the handle facing your home.
