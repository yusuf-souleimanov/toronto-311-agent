# Garbage not picked up
If your garbage bin was not emptied on your collection day, you can report a missed pickup. Solid Waste Management Services handles this.
Before reporting, check: was the bin out by the required time on the correct day, facing the street with the lid closed and nothing on top? Holidays can shift collection days.
If only your garbage was missed but recycling or the green bin was collected, say so; each stream can be collected by a different truck.
