# Illegal dumping on City roads and boulevards
Report items dumped on City property such as roadsides, boulevards, laneways and the area beside sidewalks: mattresses, furniture, bags of garbage, construction waste. Solid Waste Management Services arranges clean-up.
Describe what was dumped and exactly where. Do not open bags or touch sharp or hazardous items.
Dumping on private property is handled as a separate bylaw request type.
