# Emergencies: call 911, not 311
311 is for non-emergency City services. If anyone is in danger, call 911 right away.
Call 911 for: fire, smell of natural gas, downed or sparking power lines, a crime happening now, someone injured or unconscious, a car crash with injuries, or flooding that threatens electrical panels.
For a gas smell, leave the building first and call from outside.
After an emergency is handled, you can still report related City issues (for example a damaged road or fallen tree) through 311.
