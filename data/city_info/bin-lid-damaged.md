# Damaged bin lid
If the lid of your City-issued garbage, recycling or green bin is cracked, broken or no longer closes, you can request a repair or replacement. Solid Waste Management Services handles bin repairs.
Say which bin (garbage, recycling or green bin) and what is wrong with the lid. A damaged bin body or handle is reported as a separate request type.
A lid that doesn't close lets rain and animals in, so it is worth reporting even if the bin still works.
