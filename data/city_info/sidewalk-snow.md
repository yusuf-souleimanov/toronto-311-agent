# Sidewalk snow clearing
If a sidewalk the City clears hasn't been plowed after a snowfall, or is icy and unsafe, you can report it. Transportation Services handles sidewalk snow clearing.
The City starts clearing after a certain amount of snow has fallen, so it may take time after a storm ends.
Mention if the spot is near a school or bus stop, or is blocking people using wheelchairs or strollers. Road plowing and driveways blocked by snowbanks are separate request types.
