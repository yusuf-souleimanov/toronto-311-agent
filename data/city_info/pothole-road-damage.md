# Potholes and road damage
Report potholes, crumbling asphalt, sunken pavement and other damage on City roads. Transportation Services handles these.
Give the exact location and which lane or side of the road, and say if the hole is deep or causing cars or cyclists to swerve.
Pothole reports usually rise sharply in late winter and early spring when freeze-thaw cycles break up the road.
If your vehicle was damaged, ask 311 how to file a claim with the City.
