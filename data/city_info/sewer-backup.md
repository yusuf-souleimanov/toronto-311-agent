# Sewer backup or blocked sewer line
If sewage or water is backing up through basement floor drains or toilets, the blockage may be in the sewer line between your home and the City sewer. Toronto Water can investigate the City-owned portion.
If you can, stop using water in the house to limit the backup.
If water is near electrical outlets or the electrical panel, stay out of the area and call 911 or your utility.
A plumber can check the private part of the line; tell 311 if a plumber has already said the problem is on the City side.
