# Fallen trees and tree emergencies
Report fallen trees, large broken branches, and trees that are split or leaning dangerously after storms. The City's forestry team handles emergency clean-up of City trees.
If a tree or branch is touching power lines, stay well back and call 911 or the electricity utility; do not report it only through 311.
Say whether the tree is blocking a road, sidewalk or driveway, or has hit a vehicle or building.
