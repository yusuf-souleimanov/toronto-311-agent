# What 311 handles and how reports work
311 Toronto takes non-emergency requests for City services: roads, garbage and green bins, trees, snow clearing, noise, bylaw issues, sewers and more.
Each report becomes a service request that is sent to the City division responsible, such as Transportation Services, Solid Waste Management Services, Municipal Licensing & Standards, Toronto Water, or the forestry team.
A good report includes the type of problem, an exact location (street address, intersection, or postal code), and a short description.
Questions about things 311 reports don't cover, such as property tax payments, parking ticket disputes, licences, permits and recreation programs, are answered by other City services. A 311 agent can point you to the right place.
