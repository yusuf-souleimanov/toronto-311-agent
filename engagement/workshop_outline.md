# Client Workshop: 10 Slides

Audience: compliance manager and one IT lead. 30 minutes. No code on screen.

1. **The problem in their words.** Time and risk of manual review.
2. **What we heard.** Requirements, back to them (from the use-case canvas).
3. **Proposed solution, one picture.** Simplified architecture.
4. **Live demo.** Batch review plus one open question to the agent.
5. **Where the human stays in control.** The review policy.
6. **Does it work?** Eval table: baseline vs LLMs vs agent, in plain words.
7. **Where it fails.** 3 real failure examples. Being honest here builds trust.
8. **Governance.** Top 4 risks and their controls.
9. **What you need to have in place.** Prerequisites checklist, go/no-go.
10. **Pilot plan and next steps.** Scope, duration, success metrics.
